FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything else, just ask us.

How fast can you get started?

Most projects launch within 1–2 weeks. We start with a short discovery call to understand your goals, then send a fixed quote before any work begins.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes — every engagement starts with a free 30-minute consultation. We'll review your goals, your current site, and outline a plan that fits your budget.

What do you need from me?

Just your goals and any existing content (Lasaani, text, photos). Don't have content yet? No problem — we can help create it as part of your project.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Absolutely. Around 80% of our clients are small businesses and early-stage startups. Our pricing is transparent and there are no long-term contracts.

How can I get a quote?

Use the contact form below or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within 1–2 business days with a fixed, itemized quote.
